
# Area of a Circle

You already know how to find the area of a rectangle and a square. The area of a rectangle is its length multiplied by its width [S3]. For a square, you simply multiply the side by itself. A circle is round, so you might wonder how to measure its area. We use a special formula to find the area of the space enclosed by a circle.

## The Formula and the Value of Pi

The area of a circle is calculated using the formula $A = \pi r^2$, where $A$ stands for the area, $\pi$ (pi) is a special constant number, and $r$ is the radius [S1]. The radius is the distance from the center of the circle to any point on its edge. The value of $\pi$ is approximately $3.14159...$, and it is a non-terminating decimal [S2]. Because $\pi$ is an irrational number, we often round it to $3.14$ when doing calculations in school.

## Squaring the Radius

The $r^2$ part of the formula means you must multiply the radius by itself. This is called squaring the radius. This step is crucial because it gives us the area of a square that perfectly fits inside the circle's diameter. 

To find the area, you multiply this squared radius by $\pi$. If the radius is given in centimeters, the area will be in square centimeters ($cm^2$) [S3]. For example, if you have a circular pizza with a radius of 5 cm, you first square 5 to get 25. Then, you multiply 25 by $3.14$ to find the area of the pizza.

## Worked Example

Let us find the area of a circle with a radius of 10 cm. First, we identify the radius, which is $r = 10$. Next, we square the radius: $r^2 = 10^2 = 100$. Finally, we multiply this result by $\pi$. Using $3.14$ for $\pi$, the area is $3.14 \times 100 = 314$. Therefore, the area of the circle is $314 \, cm^2$.

## Key points

- The area of a circle is the space enclosed by its boundary, calculated using the formula $A = \pi r^2$.
- The radius ($r$) is the distance from the center of the circle to its edge.
- You must square the radius by multiplying it by itself before multiplying by $\pi$.
- The value of $\pi$ is an irrational number approximately equal to $3.14159...$.
- The unit for the area of a circle is always a square unit, such as $cm^2$, because area measures two dimensions.
