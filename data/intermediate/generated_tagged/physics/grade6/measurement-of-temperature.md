
# Measurement of Temperature

Temperature tells us how hot or cold a body is. It is a reliable measure of hotness or coldness. If one body has a higher temperature than another, it is hotter. The difference in temperature between two bodies tells us how hot one is in comparison to the other. To measure this, we use a device called a thermometer.

## What is a Thermometer?

A thermometer is a device that measures temperature. There are two main kinds of thermometers that you will come across. Clinical thermometers are used to measure human body temperatures. Laboratory thermometers are used for many other purposes in science labs.

## The Celsius Scale and Modern Thermometers

For measuring temperature, clinical thermometers generally use a scale called the Celsius scale. On this scale, the unit of temperature is the degree Celsius, which is denoted by $^{\circ}$ C.

Some older thermometers, like those used for measuring body temperature, used mercury. Mercury is an extremely toxic substance. It is also difficult to dispose of if the thermometer breaks accidentally. Digital thermometers pose no such risk. The numbers in a digital display are easier to read. Therefore, mercury thermometers are being replaced by digital thermometers. In a digital thermometer, temperature is determined with the help of heat sensors.

## Clinical Thermometers

A clinical thermometer is built for one narrow job. Human body temperature stays within a small range around 37 degrees Celsius, so a clinical thermometer only needs to cover a few degrees either side of that, and it is marked finely enough to show small changes within that range. Modern ones show the reading digitally, run on batteries, and measure when the tip is held in contact with the body.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, some special thermometers were used. These could measure the temperature of a person from a distance. They are non-contact thermometers, also called infrared thermometers. Such thermometers can measure temperature without touching a person's body. This reduces the risk of spreading disease.

## Laboratory Thermometers

There are many types of laboratory thermometers. The one in your school laboratory might look like a long, narrow, uniform glass tube that is sealed. At one end of the tube is a bulb which contains a liquid. Outside the bulb, in the tube, a narrow column of liquid can be seen. There is a Celsius scale marked along the tube. The liquid column rises or falls with a change in temperature. The mark of the Celsius scale with which the top level of the liquid column coincides is the temperature reading. The liquid used in the laboratory thermometer is generally alcohol, which is coloured red to make it easily seen, or mercury.

## Worked Example

Imagine you are checking the room temperature in your school laboratory. You hang a room thermometer on the wall. The liquid column inside the thermometer rises or falls depending on how hot or cold the air is. If the top level of the liquid column lines up exactly with the mark 30, then the room temperature is 30 degrees Celsius. The thermometer gives you an approximate idea of the room temperature.

## Key points

- Temperature is a reliable measure of how hot or cold a body is.
- A device that measures temperature is called a thermometer.
- Clinical thermometers are used to measure human body temperatures, while laboratory thermometers are used for many other purposes.
- Clinical thermometers generally use the Celsius scale, where the unit is the degree Celsius ($^{\circ}$ C).
- Mercury is toxic and hard to dispose of, so digital thermometers are replacing mercury ones.
- Digital thermometers use heat sensors to determine temperature and run on batteries.
- Infrared thermometers can measure temperature from a distance without touching the body.
- Laboratory thermometers use a sealed glass tube with a bulb and a liquid column that moves with temperature changes.
