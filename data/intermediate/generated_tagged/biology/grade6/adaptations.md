
# Adaptations

An adaptation is a special feature or behaviour that helps an organism survive in its environment. Every environment, whether it is a hot desert, a cold mountain, or deep water, has different challenges. Organisms develop specific traits to meet those challenges. These adaptations ensure that animals and plants can find food, move around, and stay safe from danger.

## Body Shapes for Moving

The way an organism moves depends on where it lives. Animals living in water have bodies that are streamlined, which means they are shaped to cut through the water easily. They use fins to help them swim. In contrast, animals living on land, such as goats, have legs to help them walk on the ground.

## Surviving in the Desert

Deserts are very hot and dry places. Plants and animals living there must adapt to survive with very little water. Desert plants often have thick stems or leaves that can store water. Some animals have special ways to stay cool. They might stay underground during the hottest part of the day. These adaptations help them save water and avoid the extreme heat.

## Living in Water

Aquatic environments, like ponds and oceans, require different adaptations. Fishes have gills to breathe underwater. They also have fins to steer and balance their bodies. Their streamlined bodies help them swim smoothly. Without these adaptations, a fish would not be able to live or move in water.

## High Mountain Adaptations

Mountain environments are cold and often have thin air. Animals living in high mountains need thick fur to keep their bodies warm. Their lungs and blood might also be adapted to take in oxygen more efficiently. These traits help them survive the freezing temperatures and lack of oxygen.

## Worked example

Let us take two animals and work out why each is suited to the place it lives in. Compare a fish with a goat. The fish has a smooth, narrow, streamlined body and fins instead of legs. Water is far harder to push through than air, so a streamlined shape lets the fish slip through it with little effort, and its fins push against the water to drive it forward and to steer. The goat has four strong legs ending in hard hooves. Land does not have to be pushed aside, but it does have to be gripped, and a hoof grips rock and loose soil well enough for a goat to climb a steep hillside. Neither animal would manage in the other's home: the fish cannot walk and the goat cannot swim far. Each body is built for the surroundings its owner lives in.

## Key points
- An adaptation is a special feature that helps an organism survive in its environment.
- Body shapes and parts, like fins or legs, are adaptations for movement.
- Desert organisms adapt to survive in hot and dry conditions.
- Aquatic organisms adapt to live and breathe underwater.
- Mountain organisms adapt to survive in cold temperatures and thin air.
