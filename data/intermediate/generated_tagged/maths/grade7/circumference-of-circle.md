# Circumference of a Circle

The circumference of a circle is the distance covered along its boundary when you go around it once [S1]. It is also called the perimeter of a circle. Knowing the circumference helps you measure the outer boundary of circular objects, much like how you might measure the outer edge of a square photo frame or a rectangular tablecloth to find out how much lace is required.

## What is $\pi$

The circumference of a circle has a special relationship with its diameter. If you measure the circumference of any circle and divide it by its diameter, the answer is always the same number [S1]. This number is called $\pi$ (pi).

The value of $\pi$ is approximately $3.14$ [S1]. You can use this value to calculate the circumference of any circle.

## The Formula

To calculate the circumference, you can use one of two formulas. The first formula uses the radius.

The circumference of a circle is twice the product of $\pi$ and the radius [S1].
$$ C = 2 \pi r $$

The second formula uses the diameter.

The circumference of a circle is the product of $\pi$ and the diameter [S1].
$$ C = \pi d $$

Here, $r$ represents the radius of the circle, and $d$ represents the diameter.

## Worked example

Let us find the circumference of a circle with a radius of $5$ cm.

First, we use the formula $C = 2 \pi r$.

Substitute the given radius into the formula:
$$ C = 2 \times \pi \times 5 $$
$$ C = 10 \pi $$

If you want to find the exact numerical value, you can substitute $\pi \approx 3.14$:
$$ C = 10 \times 3.14 $$
$$ C = 31.4 \text{ cm} $$

Therefore, the circumference of the circle is $31.4$ cm.

## Key points
- The circumference of a circle is its outer boundary, also known as its perimeter.
- The ratio of the circumference to the diameter of any circle is always $\pi$ [S1].
- The formula for circumference using the radius is $C = 2 \pi r$ [S1].
- The formula for circumference using the diameter is $C = \pi d$ [S1].
- The approximate value of $\pi$ used for calculations is $3.14$ [S1].
