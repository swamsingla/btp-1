# Surface Area of Sphere

A sphere is a three-dimensional figure, much like a ball. It is made up of all points in space that lie at a constant distance from a fixed point. This fixed point is called the centre. The constant distance from the centre to any point on the surface is called the radius, written as $r$. The surface area of a sphere tells us the total area of its outer curved surface. Understanding this concept is useful when we need to figure out how much material is needed to cover a perfectly round object, such as a ball or a globe.

## What is a Sphere?

A sphere is a solid figure. It is like the surface of a ball. To picture a sphere, think about a circle. You can draw a circle on a piece of paper because it is a plane closed figure. Every point on a circle lies at a constant distance from a fixed point, which is the centre of the circle. If you take a circular disc and rotate it around a diameter, you create a new solid. This new solid is called a sphere. When the circle rotates, its centre becomes the centre of the sphere.

## Finding the Surface Area

You may already know how to find the area of a circle. The formula for the area of a circle is $\pi r^2$. The surface area of a sphere is directly related to the area of a circle. You can imagine covering the surface of a rubber ball with a piece of string. If you wind the string around the ball until the entire surface is covered, and then unwind the string, you can use that string to fill exactly four circles. Each of these circles has the exact same radius as the ball. This means the string used to cover the sphere is exactly equal to four times the area of a circle.

The surface area of a sphere of radius $r$ is $4$ times the area of a circle of radius $r = 4 \times (\pi r^2)$. Therefore, the formula for the surface area of a sphere is $4\pi r^2$, where $r$ is the radius of the sphere.

## What is a Hemisphere?

A sphere can be divided into two equal parts. This happens when you slice the solid sphere exactly through the middle with a plane that passes through its centre. Each half is called a hemisphere. The word 'hemi' means half. A hemisphere has two faces. There is a curved face and a flat face, which is called the base. The curved surface area of a hemisphere is exactly half the surface area of the sphere. The total surface area of a hemisphere includes both the curved part and the flat base.

## Worked Example

Here is how to use the formula to find the surface area of a sphere. Let us find the surface area of a sphere with a radius of $5$ cm. We use the formula $4\pi r^2$. First, we square the radius, which is $5$. Squaring $5$ gives $25$. Next, we multiply $4$ by $\pi$. We can use $3.14$ for $\pi$ to get a simple number. Multiplying $4$ by $3.14$ gives $12.56$. Finally, we multiply $12.56$ by $25$. This calculation gives $314$. So, the surface area of the sphere is $314$ square cm.

## Key points

- A sphere is a solid figure made of all points in space at a constant distance from a fixed centre.
- The formula for the surface area of a sphere is $4\pi r^2$, where $r$ is the radius.
- A hemisphere is exactly half of a sphere.
- The curved surface area of a hemisphere is $2\pi r^2$.
- The total surface area of a hemisphere is $3\pi r^2$, which includes the flat base.
