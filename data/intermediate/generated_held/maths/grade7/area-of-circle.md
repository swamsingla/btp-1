# Area of a Circle

Have you ever wondered how to find the space inside a round pizza or a circular garden? While we know how to find the space inside a rectangle by multiplying its length and width, finding the space inside a circle requires a slightly different approach. The area of a circle is the amount of flat space it covers. It is a fundamental concept in geometry that helps us measure circular objects in everyday life.

## The Formula for Area

To find the area of a circle, we use a special formula: $A = \pi r^2$. Here, $A$ stands for the area, $\pi$ (pi) is a special number, and $r$ is the radius of the circle. The radius is the straight-line distance from the center of the circle to any point on its edge. The number $\pi$ is a constant value, which means it never changes, and it is approximately equal to $3.14$. 

Why do we square the radius? When you multiply the radius by itself, you are essentially calculating the area of a square that would fit perfectly inside the circle if the circle were transformed. This rule is a fundamental property of circles in geometry.

## Finding the Radius

Before you can use the formula $A = \pi r^2$, you need to know the radius. Sometimes, a problem might give you the diameter instead. The diameter is the straight-line distance across the circle, passing through the center. The relationship between the radius and the diameter is very simple: the diameter is exactly twice the radius. If you know the diameter, you can find the radius by dividing the diameter by $2$.

Once you have the radius, you can substitute it into the area formula. For example, if a circle has a radius of $5$ cm, you would calculate the area by substituting $5$ for $r$ in the formula.

## Worked Example

Let us apply the formula to a real problem. Imagine you have a circular garden with a radius of $7$ meters. To find the area of the grass inside the garden, you need to calculate $A = \pi r^2$. 

First, identify the radius, which is $7$ meters. Next, substitute $7$ into the formula: $A = \pi \times 7^2$. Calculate $7^2$ to get $49$. Therefore, the area of the garden is $49\pi$ square meters. If you want a decimal answer, you can multiply $49$ by $3.14$ to get approximately $153.86$ square meters.

## Key points

* The area of a circle is calculated using the formula $A = \pi r^2$.
* The radius must be known before applying the formula.
* The diameter is twice the length of the radius.
* The value of $\pi$ is a constant, approximately equal to $3.14$.
* Always square the radius before multiplying by $\pi$.
