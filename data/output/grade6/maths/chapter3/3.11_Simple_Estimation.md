## 3. Explanation  

### 3.1 What is Estimation?  
**Estimation** is the process of finding an approximate value instead of an exact one. It’s useful when you don’t need precision or when exact numbers aren’t available.  

> **Definition**: Estimation involves using round numbers or simplified calculations to find a close enough answer.  

### 3.2 When to Use Estimation  
You might use estimation in situations like:  
- Guessing how long a task will take.  
- Calculating the total cost of groceries.  
- Judging distances or quantities in daily life.  

### 3.3 Estimation Techniques  
There are two common ways to estimate:  
1. **Rounding**: Replace numbers with nearby, simpler numbers (e.g., 29 becomes 30).  
2. **Front-end estimation**: Use only the leftmost digit(s) and ignore the rest (e.g., 456 ≈ 400).  

> **Formula for rounding**: Round numbers to the nearest 10, 100, or 1000 depending on the context.

## 4. Solved Examples  

### Example 1: Estimate Total Students in School  
Paromita’s class has 32 students, and the other two classes have 29 and 35 students. Estimate the total number of students in her school.  

**Solution**:  
1. Round each class size: $ 32 \approx 30 $, $ 29 \approx 30 $, $ 35 \approx 30 $.  
2. Add rounded numbers: $ 30 + 30 + 30 = 90 $.  
3. Multiply by the number of classes (assuming 5 classes): $ 90 \times 5 = 450 $.  
**Answer**: Approximately 450 students.  

### Example 2: Estimate Walking Distance  
If you take 2 steps per meter and your classroom is 15 meters away, estimate how many steps you’ll take.  

**Solution**:  
1. Calculate steps: $ 15 \text{ meters} \times 2 \text{ steps/meter} = 30 \text{ steps} $.  
**Answer**: Approximately 30 steps.  

### Example 3: Check Roshan’s Cost Estimate  
Roshan buys milk (₹30), apples (₹20), bananas (₹15), and oranges (₹25) for 5 people. His estimate is ₹100. Is this reasonable?  

**Solution**:  
1. Total actual cost: $ 30 + 20 + 15 + 25 = ₹90 $.  
2. Divide by 5 people: $ 90 ÷ 5 = ₹18 $ per person.  
3. Total for 5 people: $ 18 \times 5 = ₹90 $.  
**Answer**: Yes, ₹100 is a fair estimate.

## 6. Summary  
- **Estimation** simplifies complex calculations by using approximate values.  
- Round numbers to the nearest 10, 100, or 1000 for ease.  
- Use estimation for tasks like budgeting, measuring, or predicting outcomes.  
- Always check if your estimate is reasonable by comparing it to known facts.

## 7. Key Formulas  

| Formula | Description |  
|--------|------------|  
| $ \text{Estimated Value} = \text{Rounded Number} $ | Round numbers to simplify calculations. |  
| $ \text{Total} \approx (\text{Sum of Rounded Values}) $ | Combine rounded numbers for totals. |  
| $ \text{Average} \approx \frac{\text{Total Estimated Value}}{\text{Number of Items}} $ | Find averages using estimates. |

## 8. Student Corner  

### Common Mistakes  
- **Overcomplicating**: Don’t use exact numbers unless required.  
- **Ignoring Units**: Always match units (e.g., meters vs. kilometers).  

### Memory Aid  
**"Round and Rule"**: Round numbers first, then perform calculations—it’s faster and easier!
