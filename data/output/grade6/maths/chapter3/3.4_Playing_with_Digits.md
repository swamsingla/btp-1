## 3. Explanation  

### 3.1 Digit Sum  
The **digit sum** of a number is the total of all its individual digits.  
> **Definition**: For any number, the digit sum is calculated by adding all its digits.  
> **Example**: For the number 345, the digit sum is $3 + 4 + 5 = 12 $.  

#### Properties of Digit Sums  
- **Consistency**: Different numbers can share the same digit sum.  
- **Range**: The digit sum of a number depends on its size. For example, the maximum digit sum for a 2-digit number is $ 9 + 9 = 18 $.  
- **Patterns**: When exploring digit sums, interesting patterns emerge, especially when considering consecutive numbers or sequences.  

### 3.2 Counting Digit Occurrences  
When analyzing numbers, we often count how many times a specific digit appears.  
> **Definition**: To count the occurrences of a digit (like 7) in a range, examine each digit position (units, tens, hundreds) separately.  

#### Step-by-Step Method to Count Digit '7'  
1. **Units Place**: In every group of 10 numbers (e.g., 1–10, 11–20), the digit 7 appears once in the units place.  
2. **Tens Place**: In every group of 100 numbers (e.g., 1–100), the digit 7 appears 10 times in the tens place (70–79).  
3. **Hundreds Place**: In every group of 1000 numbers, the digit 7 appears 100 times in the hundreds place (700–799).  

**Formula for Total Occurrences**:  
If counting digit $ d $ in numbers from $ 1 $ to $ N $, calculate contributions from each place (units, tens, hundreds, etc.).  

### 3.3 Exploring Patterns  
The source text encourages exploring patterns through activities like:  
- Finding numbers with specific digit sums.  
- Observing trends in digit sums across ranges (e.g., numbers from 40 to 70).  

**Key Insight**: Digit sums often reveal symmetries or repeating behaviors. For example, numbers with digit sum 14 may form sequences like 59 (5+9=14), 68 (6+8=14), or 77 (7+7=14).

## 4. Solved Examples  

### **Example 1**: Find numbers with digit sum 14  
**Solution**:  
To find numbers with digit sum 14, consider all possible combinations of digits that add to 14:  
- Two-digit numbers:  
  - $ 5 + 9 = 14 $ → 59  
  - $ 6 + 8 = 14 $ → 68  
  - $ 7 + 7 = 14 $ → 77  
  - $ 8 + 6 = 14 $ → 86  
  - $ 9 + 5 = 14 $ → 95  
- Three-digit numbers:  
  - $ 1 + 5 + 8 = 14 $ → 158  
  - $ 2 + 4 + 8 = 14 $ → 248  
  - Continue this process for all valid combinations.  

**Answer**: Numbers like 59, 68, 77, 86, 95, 158, 248, etc., have digit sum 14.  

---

### **Example 2**: Count occurrences of digit 7 from 1 to 100  
**Solution**:  
1. **Units Place**:  
   - In 1–10: 7 occurs once (7).  
   - In 11–20: 17 → 1 occurrence.  
   - Repeat this pattern for every 10 numbers.  
   - Total: $ 10 $ times (once per group of 10).  
2. **Tens Place**:  
   - Numbers 70–79 contain 7 in the tens place.  
   - Total: $ 10 $ times.  
3. **Total Occurrences**: $ 10 + 10 = 20 $.  

**Answer**: The digit 7 appears **20 times** from 1 to 100.  

---

### **Example 3**: Calculate digit sums of numbers from 40 to 70  
**Solution**:  
List numbers and compute their digit sums:  
- 40: $ 4 + 0 = 4 $  
- 41: $ 4 + 1 = 5 $  
- ...  
- 49: $ 4 + 9 = 13 $  
- 50: $ 5 + 0 = 5 $  
- 51: $ 5 + 1 = 6 $  
- ...  
- 59: $ 5 + 9 = 14 $  
- 60: $ 6 + 0 = 6 $  
- ...  
- 69: $ 6 + 9 = 15 $  
- 70: $ 7 + 0 = 7 $  

**Observation**: The digit sums increase gradually, with higher values near the upper end of the range.  

**Answer**: The digit sums range from 4 to 15, showing a consistent upward trend.

## 5. Practice Problems  

### 🟢 Easy  
**Problem 1**: Write three numbers with digit sum 12.  
**Problem 2**: How many times does the digit 5 appear from 1 to 50?  

### 🟡 Medium  
**Problem 3**: Calculate the digit sums of all numbers from 80 to 95.  
**Problem 4**: What is the smallest 3-digit number with digit sum 15?  

### 🔴 Hard  
**Problem 5**: If a number has digit sum 20, what is the largest 5-digit number possible?  

---

### Answers  

#### Problem 1:  
Possible numbers: 39 ($ 3 + 9 = 12 $), 48 ($ 4 + 8 = 12 $), 57 ($ 5 + 7 = 12 $).  

#### Problem 2:  
- Units place: 5 occurs in 5, 15, 25, 35, 45, 50 (5 times).  
- Tens place: 50–59 contains 5 in the tens place (10 times).  
- **Total**: $ 5 + 10 = 15 $ times.  

#### Problem 3:  
Digit sums from 80–95:  
- 80: 8, 81: 9, ..., 89: 17; 90: 9, 91: 10, ..., 95: 14.  

#### Problem 4:  
Smallest 3-digit number: 69 ($ 6 + 9 = 15 $).  

#### Problem 5:  
Largest 5-digit number: 99999 (sum: $ 9 \times 5 = 45 $). Wait, but digit sum is 20.  
Correct approach: Distribute digits to maximize the number. The largest number would be 99200 (9 + 9 + 2 + 0 + 0 = 20).

## 8. Student Corner  

### **Common Mistakes**  
1. **Miscounting Digit Positions**: Forgetting to separate units, tens, and hundreds places when counting digit occurrences.  
   - ✅ Correction: Analyze each place independently.  
2. **Ignoring Leading Zeros**: Numbers like 059 are invalid (leading zeros aren’t counted).  
   - ✅ Correction: Only consider valid numbers (e.g., 59 instead of 059).  

### **Exam Tips**  
- Always verify your answers by checking smaller ranges first (e.g., count 7s from 1–10 before 1–100).  
- Use systematic methods for digit sums (e.g., list all combinations of digits that add to the target sum).  

### **Memory Aid**  
**"Digits Add Up – Think Place by Place!"**  
Break down numbers into units, tens, hundreds, etc., to simplify calculations.
