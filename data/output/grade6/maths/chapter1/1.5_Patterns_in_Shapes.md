## **Introduction**

Have you ever noticed how things around us repeat themselves? Like, did you know that the shape of a honeycomb is made up of hexagons? Or that the tiles on a football field are arranged in a specific pattern? In mathematics, we study these repeating patterns, which are called **patterns in shapes**. In this topic, we'll explore what patterns in shapes are, why they're important, and how they relate to number sequences.

## **Explanation**

### Definition and Importance

A **pattern in shapes** is a sequence of similar shapes that repeat themselves in a particular order. These patterns can be found in various areas of mathematics, such as geometry, and are used to describe the arrangement of objects in nature, art, and architecture.

### Key Terms

* **Geometry**: The branch of mathematics that studies patterns in shapes.
* **Shape sequence**: A sequence of similar shapes that repeat themselves in a particular order.

### Formulae

For a regular polygon with n sides, the formula to calculate its area is:

$$

A = \frac{n \times s^2}{4 \tan(\pi/n)}

$$

where $n $ is the number of sides, and $ s $ is the length of each side.
