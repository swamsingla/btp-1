### Introduction

Have you ever noticed that there are many interesting patterns hidden in the world around us? Let's explore one such pattern related to number sequences. A sequence is a set of numbers arranged in a particular order. We'll discover how different sequences can be connected to each other in surprising ways.

### Explanation

#### **Definition of Sequence**

A **sequence** is an ordered list of numbers, which means every number has a position in the list. For example, the sequence 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 is a sequence because each number has a specific position.

#### **Relations among Number Sequences**

Two sequences are said to be **related** if one is obtained by adding or subtracting a fixed constant to the other. In other words, if we have two sequences {a*n} and {b_n}, then they are related if there exists a constant c such that b_n = a*(n+c). This relation can be visualized using a picture.

For instance, let's consider two sequences: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. These two sequences are related because if we shift the second sequence by 1, we obtain the first sequence.

> **Theorem:** Two sequences {a*n} and {b_n} are related if there exists a constant c such that b_n = a*(n+c).

#### Key Concepts

- **Arithmetic Progression (AP)**: A sequence of numbers in which the difference between any two successive members is constant.
- **Geometric Progression (GP)**: A sequence of numbers in which the ratio between any two successive members is constant.

$$

$$

$$

$$

$$

2.  **Example 2:** Given the sequence 3, 6, 9,?,?,?, find the missing terms.

    The given sequence is an arithmetic progression with a common difference of 3. To find the missing terms, we need to add 3 successively to the last term.

$$

a_{n+1} = a_n + 3


$$

$$

a_4 = a_3 + 3 = 12


$$

$$

a_5 = a_4 + 3 = 15


$$

$$

a_6 = a_5 + 3 = 18


$$

    So, the complete sequence is 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18.

### Practice Problems

1.  **Example 3:** Find the next four terms in the sequence: 1, 4, 9, 16,?

$$

a_{n+1} = a_n + 3^2

a_5 = a_4 + 3^2 = 25 + 9 = 34

2.  **Example 4:** Find the next two terms in the sequence: 1, 2, 4, 8,?

    The given sequence is a geometric progression with a common ratio of 2. To find the next two terms, we need to multiply the last term by 2.

$$

a_{n+1} = 2a_n


$$

$$

a_4 = 2a_3 = 8


$$

$$

a_5 = 2a_4 = 16


$$

$$

a_6 = 2a_5 = 32


$$

    So, the next two terms in the sequence are 16, 32.

### Summary

- An arithmetic progression (AP) is a sequence of numbers in which the difference between any two successive members is constant.
- A geometric progression (GP) is a sequence of numbers in which the ratio between any two successive members is constant.
- The nth term of an AP can be found using the formula:

$$

a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d


$$

, where d is the common difference.

- The nth term of a GP can be found using the formula:

$$

a_n = a_1r^{(n-1)}


$$

, where r is the common ratio.

### Common Mistakes

- Not calculating the common difference or ratio correctly.
- Forgetting to adjust the index of the last term.

### Key Vocabulary

- **Arithmetic Progression (AP)**: A sequence of numbers in which the difference between any two successive members is constant.
- **Geometric Progression (GP)**: A sequence of numbers in which the ratio between any two successive members is constant.
- **Common Difference**: The constant difference between any two successive terms in an AP.
- **Common Ratio**: The constant ratio between any two successive terms in a GP.
