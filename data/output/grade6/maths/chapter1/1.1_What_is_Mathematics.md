## 2. Introduction  
Mathematics is like solving a puzzle—it helps us discover hidden patterns in the world around us! From the way trees grow to the rhythm of music, patterns are everywhere. In this lesson, you’ll learn how patterns in numbers and shapes form the foundation of mathematics and how they connect to real-life situations. By the end, you’ll understand why patterns matter and how they help us solve problems creatively.  

---  

## 3. Explanation  

### 3.1 What Are Patterns?  
Patterns are repeating arrangements or sequences that follow a rule. These can be in numbers, shapes, or even events. For example:  
- **Number Pattern**: $2, 4, 6, 8, 10 $ (each number increases by 2)  
- **Shape Pattern**: Triangle, square, triangle, square… (alternating shapes)  

Mathematics explores these patterns to understand *why* they happen and how they can be applied.  

### 3.2 Patterns in Numbers  
Numbers often form sequences based on rules. Common types include:  
- **Arithmetic Sequence**: Each term increases/decreases by a constant difference. Example: $ 3, 7, 11, 15 $ (difference = 4)  
- **Geometric Sequence**: Each term is multiplied by a constant ratio. Example: $ 2, 6, 18, 54 $ (ratio = 3)  

**Tip**: To find the next term in a sequence, identify the rule governing the pattern.  

### 3.3 Visualising Number Sequences  
Visual tools like number lines or grids help see patterns clearly. For example:  
- Plotting $ 1, 3, 5, 7 $ on a number line shows evenly spaced dots, indicating an arithmetic sequence.  
- Arranging squares in rows (like a grid) reveals patterns in area or perimeter calculations.  

### 3.4 Relations Among Number Sequences  
Sequences can overlap or interact. For instance:  
- The Fibonacci sequence ($ 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 $) appears in nature (e.g., flower petals) and is linked to ratios in geometry.  
- Prime numbers (numbers divisible only by 1 and themselves) form unique patterns with composite numbers.  

### 3.5 Patterns in Shapes  
Shapes can repeat in predictable ways:  
- Triangles arranged in rows: Row 1 (1 triangle), Row 2 (3 triangles), Row 3 (5 triangles)...  
- Circles placed in concentric rings: Ring 1 (1 circle), Ring 2 (3 circles), Ring 3 (5 circles)...  

These shape patterns often relate to number sequences.

## 4. Solved Examples  

### Example 1: Identifying a Number Pattern  
**Question**: Find the next number in the sequence: $ 5, 10, 15, 20, ?$  
**Solution**:  
- The difference between consecutive terms is $ 10 - 5 = 5 $, $ 15 - 10 = 5 $, etc.  
- Rule: Add 5 to the previous term.  
- Next term: $ 20 + 5 = 25 $.  
**Answer**: $ 25 $  

### Example 2: Visual Pattern with Shapes  
**Question**: How many triangles are in the 5th row of this pattern?  
Row 1: 1 triangle  
Row 2: 3 triangles  
Row 3: 5 triangles  
**Solution**:  
- The pattern increases by 2 triangles per row.  
- Formula for nth row: $ 2n - 1 $  
- For $ n = 5 $: $ 2(5) - 1 = 9 $  
**Answer**: 9 triangles  

### Example 3: Relating Shapes to Numbers  
**Question**: If the 4th ring of a circular pattern has 7 circles, how many circles are in the 6th ring?  
**Solution**:  
- The pattern starts with 1 circle in the 1st ring and adds 2 circles each time.  
- Sequence: $ 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 $  
- 6th ring: $ 2(6) - 1 = 11 $  
**Answer**: 11 circles

## 5. Practice Problems  

🟢 **Easy**: Continue the sequence: $ 4, 8, 12, ?, ?$  
🟡 **Medium**: Draw the 4th figure in this shape pattern: △, △△, △△△, ...  
🔴 **Hard**: If the 3rd row of a triangular pattern has 7 tiles, how many tiles are in the 5th row?  

**Answers**:  
- **Easy**: Next terms are $ 16 $ and $ 20 $ (rule: add 4).  
- **Medium**: The 4th figure has 4 triangles (△△△△).  
- **Hard**: Formula: $ 2n + 1 $. For $ n = 5 $: $ 2(5) + 1 = 11 $ tiles.

## 6. Summary  
- Mathematics discovers patterns in numbers, shapes, and real-world phenomena.  
- Patterns follow rules like addition, multiplication, or geometric growth.  
- Visual tools (number lines, grids) help analyze patterns.  
- Understanding patterns leads to practical applications in science, technology, and daily life.
