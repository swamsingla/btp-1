## Explanation

Drawing angles involves measuring and marking the degree measure on a straight line. To start, we need a reference point – the **vertex** of the angle. We'll use a **ray** as our guide to ensure accuracy.

> In order to draw an angle accurately, it is essential to understand that the sum of the interior angles of a triangle is always 180°. </blockquote>

### Key Concepts:

*   **Vertex**: The point where two rays meet.
*   **Ray**: An imaginary line extending infinitely from a single point.
*   **Angle**: A figure formed by two rays sharing a common endpoint called the **vertex**.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Drawing Angles

To draw an angle with a specific measure, follow these steps:

1.  Place the vertex on the paper where you want the angle to begin.
2.  Extend one of the rays until it reaches a convenient mark or edge.
3.  Open your protractor and set it to the desired angle measure.
4.  Align the center of the protractor with the vertex.
5.  Count the degrees along the protractor, marking the point where you stop at the desired measure.
6.  Join the two ends of the ray with a line segment to complete the angle.

## Student Corner

*   **Common Mistake:** Incorrectly aligning the protractor with the vertex, leading to inaccurate measurements.
*   **Correction:** Double-check the alignment before starting to draw the angle.
