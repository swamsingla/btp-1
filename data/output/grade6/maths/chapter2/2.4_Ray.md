## Introduction
Can you imagine drawing a straight line without a pencil? It's easy, right? But what about drawing a line that keeps going forever? That's called a **ray** (denoted by the symbol →). In this topic, we'll explore rays, angles, and how they're connected. Get ready to extend your knowledge of geometry!

We'll review what you've learned so far in this chapter and beyond, and discover new ways to measure and work with lines and angles.

## Explanation
### What is a Ray?

A ray is a part of a line that starts at one point, called the **initial point**, and extends infinitely in a single direction. Think of it like a beam of light shining out from a lighthouse or a sunbeam stretching across the sky.

> **Definition:** A ray is a segment of a line that has a fixed initial point and extends infinitely in a single direction.

### Key Terms

*   **Initial point**: The point from which a ray originates.
*   **Direction**: The way a ray extends.
*   **Line segment**: A part of a line between two distinct points.

### Important Properties

*   A ray always has a start point and extends indefinitely in one direction.
*   Rays are used to describe lines extending infinitely.

## Summary

•   A ray is a segment of a line with a fixed initial point and extends infinitely in one direction.
•   Rays are used to describe lines extending infinitely.
•   The direction of a ray is determined by the initial point.
•   Distance between two points on a ray can be calculated using the coordinate difference.

### Key Formulas

*

$$

d_{AB} = \left| x_B - x_A \right|

$$
*

$$

\text{Distance} = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}

$$

### Exam Tips

*   When working with rays, focus on identifying the initial point and direction.
*   Use coordinate geometry to calculate distances between points.

## Student Corner
### Common Mistakes

*   Not distinguishing between rays and lines
*   Forgetting to include the initial point

### Memory Aid

*   To recall the definition of a ray, think of it as a beam of light extending from a source.

### Did You Know?

*   The shortest distance between two points is along a straight line, including a ray!
