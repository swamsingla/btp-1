**2.9 Measuring Angles**

Do you know how to measure an angle without comparing it to another angle?

Imagine you're trying to determine the size of a room. Can you just look at it and tell how big it is? No, but mathematicians found a way to quantify the size of an angle!

We'll explore this method using a special tool called a protractor.

### Explanation

An **angle** is formed by two rays sharing the same endpoint called the **vertex**.

The **degree measure** of an angle is the number of 1-degree units (called **unit parts**) that fit inside the angle.

The number of unit parts is determined by dividing the angle into equal parts, usually 360.

Each unit part represents 1 degree.

The **angle measure** of an angle is the total number of unit parts it contains.

For instance, ∠KAL has 30 unit parts, so its angle measure is 30°.

### Key Concepts

> **Definition of an Angle**: An angle is formed by two rays sharing the same endpoint (vertex).
>
> **Definition of Degree Measure**: The number of 1-degree units (unit parts) that fit inside an angle.
>
> **Definition of Unit Part**: A 1-degree unit is the basic unit of measurement for angles.
>
> **Definition of Angle Measure**: The total number of unit parts within an angle.

### Important Formulas

$$

\text{Angle Measure} = \frac{\text{Number of Units}}{\text{Total Number of Units}} \times 360^\circ

$$

$$

\text{Number of Units} = \frac{\text{Angle Measure}}{360^\circ}

$$

### Solved Examples

#### Example 1:

A protractor shows that ∠ABC has 90 unit parts. What is its angle measure?

$$

\text{Angle Measure} = \frac{90 \text{ units}}{360^\circ} \times 360^\circ = 90^\circ

$$

#### Example 2:

If a protractor indicates that ∠DEF has 180 unit parts, what is its angle measure?

$$

\text{Angle Measure} = \frac{180 \text{ units}}{360^\circ} \times 360^\circ = 180^\circ

$$

#### Example 3:

Using a protractor, calculate the angle measure of ∠GHI.

Since ∠GHI is a straight angle, it has 180 unit parts.

$$

\text{Angle Measure} = \frac{180 \text{ units}}{360^\circ} \times 360^\circ = 180^\circ

$$

### Practice Problems

1. Find the angle measure of ∠JKL using a protractor. (🟢 Easy)

2. Calculate the angle measure of ∠MNO using a protractor. (🟡 Medium)

3. Determine the angle measure of ∠PQR using a protractor. (🔴 Hard)

### Answers

1. 45°
2. 135°
3. 225°

### Summary

*   Recall that the angle measure is the number of unit parts within an angle.
*   To find the number of unit parts, divide the angle measure by 360.
*   The unit part is the basic unit of measurement for angles.

### Common Mistakes

*   Confusing the term "angle measure" with "angle size."

### Exam Tips

*   Always read the protractor carefully before measuring an angle.
*   Count the number of unit parts accurately.

### Did You Know?

The ancient civilizations of Babylon, Egypt, and India used 360 as the number of degrees in a circle because it has unique properties that make it easy to divide into smaller units (like 60, 30, and 10).
