## Introduction
### A Comparative Analysis of Mouths

Have you ever wondered why the size of the opening of a mouth can vary greatly among different animals? This variation is due to the difference in the angles formed by the jaws. Just as we can compare the angles formed by the jaws of different animals, we can also compare two angles in general. But how?

In this chapter, we'll explore the concept of comparing angles using the technique called **superimposition**, which involves overlaying two angles on top of each other to determine their relative sizes.

**What Students Already Know:** In our previous lessons, we've learned about **rays**, **angles**, and **vertices**. We've also explored the concept of measuring angles using a protractor.

## Explanation
### What are Angles?

An **angle** is formed by two rays sharing a common vertex.

$$

\angle AOB = \begin{cases}
\text{measured counterclockwise}, & \text{if } A \text{ is clockwise from } O \\ 
\text{measured clockwise}, & \text{if } A \text{ is counterclockwise from } O 
\end{cases}

$$

### Superimposition Method

When two angles are superimposed, the vertices coincide, and the two rays overlap.

Using this method, we can easily compare the sizes of two angles.

> **Theorem:** When two angles are superimposed, the size of one angle is greater than the other if the corresponding ray of the larger angle lies outside the smaller angle.

### Properties of Angles

*   **Reflex Angle**: An angle whose measure is greater than 180°.
*   **Straight Angle**: An angle whose measure is exactly 180°.
*   **Right Angle**: An angle whose measure is exactly 90°.

$$

\angle AOB < 90^\circ \Rightarrow \angle AOB \text{ is acute}

$$

$$

\angle AOB > 90^\circ \Rightarrow \angle AOB \text{ is obtuse}

$$

### Example 1:

Compare the angles ∠ABC and ∠XYZ.

$$

\begin{aligned}
&\text{Step 1: Superimpose the angles}\\
&\text{Step 2: Determine the size of each angle}\\
&\text{Step 3: Compare the sizes}\\
\end{aligned}

$$

**Solution:**

∠ABC ≈ 30°

∠XYZ ≈ 60°

∠XYZ > ∠ABC

\end{aligned}

### Medium

## Answers
### Easy

1.  ∠DEF > ∠ABC
2.  ∠MNP > ∠JKL
3.  ∠PQR > ∠MNO

### Medium

1.  ∠VWUX > ∠WXYZ
2.  ∠MNP = ∠JKL
3.  ∠TUVW = ∠PQRS

### Hard

1.  ∠XYZ < ∠ABC
2.  ∠QRST = ∠MNOP
3.  ∠JKL < ∠FGHI

## Student Corner
### Common Mistakes

*   Not considering the direction of the rays when comparing angles.
*   Failing to account for the size of the angle when determining equality.

### Exam Tips

*   Always ensure that the rays of the angles are properly oriented when applying the superimposition method.
*   Verify that the vertices of the angles align correctly when comparing their sizes.

### Memory Aid

*   "Superimposition": a technique for comparing the sizes of two angles by overlaying them.
