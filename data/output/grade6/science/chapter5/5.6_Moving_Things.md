### Explanation

#### Definition of Key Terms

* **Motion**: A change in an object's position over time.
* **At Rest**: An object that is not changing its position over time.
* **Velocity**: Speed in a specific direction.

> **Theorem:** An object is either at rest or in motion.

#### Moving Things

To determine if an object is in motion or at rest, we need to observe its position relative to a fixed point. If the object's position changes with respect to that point over time, it's considered in motion. Conversely, if the object's position remains unchanged, it's considered at rest.

#### Formulas

$$

v = \frac{\text{distance traveled}}{\text{time taken}}

$$

$$

d = v \times t

$$

where $v $ is velocity, $ d $ is distance, and $ t $ is time.

### Solved Examples

**Easy Example 1:**

A car travels from point A to point B at a constant speed of 50 km/h. If it takes 2 hours to cover the distance, find its average velocity.

1. Calculate the total distance traveled.
2. Find the average velocity using the formula:

$$

v = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}

$$
3. Simplify the expression to get the final answer.

Solution:

$$

d = 50 \, \text{km/h} \times 2 \, \text{hours} = 100 \, \text{km}

$$

$$

v = \frac{100 \, \text{km}}{2 \, \text{hours}} = 50 \, \text{km/h}

$$

**Medium Example 2:**

A bicycle travels from point P to point Q at an initial velocity of 20 m/s and then accelerates uniformly to a final velocity of 30 m/s in 10 seconds. Find its average velocity.

1. Find the distance traveled during acceleration.
2. Use the formula:

$$

v_f = v_i + a \times t

$$ to find the acceleration.
3. Calculate the average velocity using the formula:

$$

v_{avg} = \frac{v_i + v_f}{2}

$$

Solution:

$$

d = v_i \times t + \frac{1}{2} \cdot a \cdot t^2

$$

$$

30 = 20 + a \cdot 10

$$
$$

a = 1 \, \text{m/s}^2

$$

$$

d = 20 \cdot 10 + \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1 \cdot 10^2 = 200 \, \text{m}

$$
$$

v_{avg} = \frac{20 + 30}{2} = 25 \, \text{m/s}

$$

**Hard Example 3:**

A particle moves along a straight line under the influence of gravity. Its displacement at time $t=0 $ is $ x(0)=0 $. At $ t=t_1 $, its velocity is $ v(t_1)=10 \, \text{m/s} $ and at $ t=t_2 $, its velocity is $ v(t_2)=-15 \, \text{m/s} $. Find its average velocity over the interval $\left[0,t_2\right] $.

1. Use the equation of motion:

$$

x(t) = x_0 + v_0t + \frac{1}{2}at^2

$$ to find the position at $t=t_2 $.
2. Calculate the displacement over the interval $\left[0,t_2\right] $.
3. Find the average velocity using the formula:

$$

v_{avg} = \frac{x(t_2) - x(0)}{t_2 - 0}

$$

Solution:

$$

x(t) = 0 + 10t - \frac{1}{2}\cdot 9.8\cdot t^2

$$

$$

x(t_2) = 0 + 10t_2 - 4.9t_2^2

$$
$$

d = x(t_2) - x(0) = 10t_2 - 4.9t_2^2

$$

$$

v_{avg} = \frac{10t_2 - 4.9t_2^2}{t_2}

$$

### Practice Problems

1.  A car travels from point A to point B at an initial velocity of 40 km/h and then decelerates uniformly to a final velocity of 20 km/h in 10 minutes. Find its average velocity.
   [🟢] 
   (Answer: 20 km/h)
2.  A particle moves along a straight line under the influence of gravity. Its displacement at time $t=0 $ is $ x(0)=10 $ m. At $ t=t_1 $, its velocity is $ v(t_1)=5 $ m/s and at $ t=t_2 $, its velocity is $ v(t_2)=-10 $ m/s. Find its average velocity over the interval $\left[0,t_2\right] $.
   [🟡]
   (Answer: -5 m/s)
3.  A bicycle travels from point P to point Q at a constant velocity of 20 m/s. If it takes 5 seconds to cover the distance, find its distance traveled.
   [🔴]
   (Answer: 100 m)

### Summary

*   Average velocity is calculated using the formula:

$$

v_{avg} = \frac{x(t_2) - x(0)}{t_2 - 0}

$$
*   To determine if an object is in motion or at rest, check if its position changes with respect to a fixed point over time.
*   Velocity and distance are related by the formula:

$$

d = v \times t

$$

### Student Corner

*   **Common Mistake:** Misinterpreting the concept of velocity as the same as speed. Remember that velocity has both magnitude and direction!
*   Tip: When calculating average velocity, always remember to include the change in position and time!
