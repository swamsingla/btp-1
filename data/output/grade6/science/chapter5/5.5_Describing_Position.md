## Explanation

### What is Describing Position?

Describing position refers to finding an object's location in relation to a fixed point, known as a **reference point**. Imagine you're standing in front of a lamp post, and you want to measure the distance from the post to a nearby tree. You'd use the lamp post as your reference point.

A reference point can be any object, like a wall, a building, or even a landmark. By fixing the reference point, you can easily compare the distance between objects.

### Why is Describing Position Important?

Understanding describing position is vital in various situations:

*   Navigation: To find your way through unfamiliar territories, you need to know your position relative to landmarks.
*   Physics: Describing position helps us analyze motion, velocity, and acceleration.
*   Everyday Life: Measuring distances between objects is essential in construction, architecture, and even gaming.

### Key Concepts and Formulas

*   **Distance**: The length between two points, usually measured along a straight line.
*   **Position**: The location of an object relative to a reference point.
*   Theorem: If two objects are collinear (lie on the same line), the shortest distance between them is along that line.

 Formula:

$$

d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}

$$

Where d is the distance between points $(x_1, y_1) $ and $ (x_2, y_2) $.

## Solved Examples

### Example 1:

Find the distance between Deepa's house and the bus stop, given that they are 45 m apart.

**Solution:**

No calculations needed here, as the distance is already given.

### Example 2:

Padma is traveling by bus and sees a kilometre stone indicating 'Delhi 60 km'. Find her current position.

**Solution:**

Since the kilometre stone indicates her distance from Delhi, her position is 60 km from Delhi.

### Example 3:

A basketball hoop is 15 m above the ground, and the ball is thrown from the edge of the court. If the ball travels 20 m horizontally, how far is it from the edge of the court?

**Solution:**

Using the Pythagorean theorem:

$$

d = \sqrt{(20)^2 + (15)^2} = \sqrt{400 + 225} = \sqrt{625} = 25

$$

The ball is 25 m away from the edge of the court.
