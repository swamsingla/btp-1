**Standard Units**
================

### Introduction
Do you know how long it takes to walk from your house to school? Or how far away your favorite park is from your home? To answer these questions, we need to measure distances. But did you ever wonder why we don't just use a single unit of measurement for everything? Let's find out!

In this topic, we'll learn about the importance of standard units and how they help us measure lengths accurately. We'll also discover the different units used to measure length, including meters, centimeters, millimeters, kilometers, and inches. By the end of this topic, you'll understand the concept of standard units and how to apply them to real-life situations.

### Explanation
#### Definition of Standard Units
A **standard unit** is a unit of measurement that is widely accepted and used across different countries and regions. It ensures that measurements are consistent and accurate, making it easier to compare quantities and perform calculations.

The International System of Units (SI units) is the most commonly used system of measurement globally. The SI unit of length is the **meter** (m), denoted by the symbol'm'. One meter is further divided into 100 equal divisions, known as **centimeters** (cm), and 1000 divisions, known as **millimeters** (mm).

#### Key Properties of Centimeters and Millimeters

$$
\text{Length of a section between two consecutive marks on a centimeter scale} = 1 \text{ cm}

$$

$$
\text{Length of a section between two consecutive marks on a millimeter scale} = 1 \text{ mm}

$$

One centimeter is further divided into 10 equal parts, and one millimeter is equal to one-tenth of a centimeter.

#### Importance of Standard Units
Using standard units helps us to:

*   Compare quantities easily
*   Perform calculations accurately
*   Communicate effectively with others
*   Make precise measurements in various fields, such as science, engineering, and construction

#### Example 1: Converting Between Units
Convert 12.5 cm to meters:

1.  Divide 12.5 by 100 to convert centimeters to meters:

$$

\frac{12.5}{100} = 0.125 \text{ m}

$$

#### Example 2: Converting Between Units
Convert 250 mm to centimeters:

1.  Multiply 250 by 10 to convert millimeters to centimeters:

$$

250 \times 10 = 2500 \text{ cm}

$$

#### Example 3: Converting Between Units
Convert 35.75 kilometers to meters:

1.  Multiply 35.75 by 1000 to convert kilometers to meters:

$$

35.75 \times 1000 = 35750 \text{ m}

$$

#### Additional Information
The kilometer is a larger unit of length, equal to 1000 meters. The inch is a smaller unit of length, equal to 2.54 centimeters.

### Solved Examples
#### Example 1:
What is the length of a 30-centimeter ruler?

1.  Divide 30 by 10 to convert centimeters to tens:

$$

\frac{30}{10} = 3

$$
2.  Multiply 3 by 10 to convert tens to millimeters:

$$

3 \times 10 = 30 \text{ mm}

$$

#### Example 2:
What is the distance traveled by a car that travels 45 kilometers in 3 hours?

1.  Multiply 45 by 1000 to convert kilometers to meters:

$$

45 \times 1000 = 45000 \text{ m}

$$
2.  Divide 45000 by 3 to convert meters to meters per hour:

$$

\frac{45000}{3} = 15000 \text{ m/h}

$$

#### Example 3:
What is the length of a 20-inch ruler?

1.  Multiply 20 by 2.54 to convert inches to centimeters:

$$

20 \times 2.54 = 50.8 \text{ cm}

$$
