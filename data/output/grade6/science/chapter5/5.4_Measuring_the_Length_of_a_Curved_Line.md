### Explanation

A curved line is a shape that has no beginning or end. Measuring the length of such a curve can be challenging, but there are ways to tackle it.

#### Key Concepts:

*   **Perimeter**: The distance around a shape, which helps us calculate the length of a curve.
*   **Arc Length**: A segment of a circle or ellipse that can be used to approximate the length of a curved line.
*   **Flexible Measuring Tape**: A tool used to measure lengths, especially in situations where traditional measuring techniques aren't feasible.

The perimeter of a polygon can be calculated by summing up the lengths of its sides. However, when dealing with curves, we can use the arc length method to estimate the total length.

To find the length of a curved line, we can divide it into smaller segments and calculate the perimeter of each one.

$$

L = \sum_{i=1}^{n} s_i

$$

where L is the total length, n is the number of segments, and $s_i $ represents the length of each segment.

Alternatively, we can use the arc length formula for a circular sector:

$$

L = \theta r

$$

where L is the arc length, θ is the central angle in radians, and r is the radius of the circle.

#### Important Formulae:

> The arc length of a circular sector is given by:
>
>

$$

L = \theta r

$$
>
> where L is the arc length, θ is the central angle in radians, and r is the radius of the circle.

> The circumference of a circle is given by:
>
>

$$

C = 2\pi r

$$
>
> where C is the circumference and r is the radius of the circle.

### Solved Examples

**Example 1:** A string of lights is stretched across a circular arch with a diameter of 10 meters. If the string covers 80% of the circumference, how long is the string?

$$

L = \frac{80}{100} \times 2\pi \times 5 = 8\pi

$$

**Example 2:** A flexible measuring tape is used to measure the length of a curved roof that follows a parabolic shape. The tape reads 12 meters at x = 0 and 24 meters at x = 2. What is the equation of the curve?

$$

y = ax^2 + bx + c

$$

Using the two points (0, 12) and (2, 24), we can set up equations and solve for a, b, and c:

$$

12 = a(0)^2 + b(0) + c

$$

$$

24 = a(2)^2 + b(2) + c

$$

Solving the system of equations yields:

$$

a = 3

$$

$$

b = 4

$$
$$

c = 9

$$

So the equation of the curve is:

$$

y = 3x^2 + 4x + 9

$$

**Example 3:** A car travels along a curved road that follows a circular path. The driver uses a speedometer to record her speed over time. If the speed increases from 40 km/h to 60 km/h in 5 seconds, what is the average speed?

$$

V_{avg} = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}

$$

$$

V_{avg} = \frac{(60 - 40)}{5} = 8 \text{ km/h}

$$

### Summary

*   The length of a curved line can be estimated using the arc length formula.
*   We can also use the perimeter of a polygon to approximate the length of a curve.
*   Flexible measuring tapes can be used to measure the length of curved shapes.
