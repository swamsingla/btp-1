Coordinate Geometry is a powerful tool that bridges algebra and geometry, allowing us to analyze shapes and figures using numerical coordinates. This chapter introduces the foundational concept of the Cartesian system, where points are represented as ordered pairs on a grid formed by perpendicular axes. By understanding how to plot points, identify quadrants, and measure distances, you’ll gain insights into spatial relationships and problem-solving techniques. These skills will prepare you for advanced topics like lines, slopes, and transformations, while also connecting to real-world applications such as maps and data visualization. Get ready to explore the elegant world of coordinates!
