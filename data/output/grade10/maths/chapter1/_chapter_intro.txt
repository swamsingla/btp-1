This chapter explores the world of real numbers, focusing on their fundamental properties and relationships. Students will revisit concepts like rational and irrational numbers, uncovering how they form the complete set of real numbers. A key highlight is the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic, which reveals the unique prime factorization of integers. Through these topics, learners will strengthen their ability to analyze numerical structures and solve complex problems. This foundational knowledge prepares them for advanced mathematical explorations.
