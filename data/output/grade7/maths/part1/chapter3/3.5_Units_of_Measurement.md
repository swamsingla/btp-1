## 3. Explanation  

### 3.1 Units of Length  
**Millimeter (mm)** is a smaller unit than **centimeter (cm)**, and **meter (m)** is larger than **centimeter**. Here’s how they relate:

$$
1 \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ mm}, \quad 1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm}

$$  
This means:  
- To convert **mm to cm**, divide by $10 $:

$$
  x \text{ mm} = \frac{x}{10} \text{ cm}

$$  
- To convert **cm to m**, divide by $100 $:

$$
  y \text{ cm} = \frac{y}{100} \text{ m}

$$  

> **Tip**: Think of a ruler: each centimeter mark is divided into 10 millimeters.  

### 3.2 Converting Between Units  
#### Example 1: Millimeters to Centimeters  
If a pen is $12 \text{ mm} $ long, how many centimeters is that?

$$
12 \text{ mm} = \frac{12}{10} \text{ cm} = 1.2 \text{ cm}

$$  
#### Example 2: Centimeters to Meters  
A classroom is $36 \text{ cm} $ tall. What is its height in meters?

$$
36 \text{ cm} = \frac{36}{100} \text{ m} = 0.36 \text{ m}

$$  

### 3.3 Real-World Applications  
The source text highlights fascinating tiny measurements:  
- Human hair: $0.1 \text{ mm} $ thick  
- Newspaper: $ 0.05 \text{ mm} $ to $ 0.08 \text{ mm} $ thick  
- Hummingbird eggs: $ 1.3 \text{ cm} $ long  
These examples show how precise units matter in science and technology!

## 4. Solved Examples  

### **Example 1:**  
Convert $ 5 \text{ mm} $ to centimeters.  
**Solution:**

$$
5 \text{ mm} = \frac{5}{10} \text{ cm} = 0.5 \text{ cm}

$$  
✅ Answer: $0.5 \text{ cm} $  

### **Example 2:**  
Convert $ 12 \text{ mm} $ to centimeters.  
**Solution:**

$$
12 \text{ mm} = \frac{12}{10} \text{ cm} = 1.2 \text{ cm}

$$  
✅ Answer: $1.2 \text{ cm} $

Convert $ 5.6 \text{ cm} $ to millimeters.  
**Solution:**

$$

**Solution:**

✅ Answer: $0.15 \text{ m} $  

### **Example 5:**  
Convert $ 325 \text{ cm} $ to meters.  
**Solution:**

$$
325 \text{ cm} = \frac{325}{100} \text{ m} = 3.25 \text{ m}

$$  
✅ Answer: $3.25 \text{ m} $

## 7. Summary  
- $ 1 \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ mm} $  
- $ 1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm} $  
- To convert mm to cm: Divide by $ 10 $  
- To convert cm to m: Divide by $ 100 $  
- To convert cm to mm: Multiply by $ 10 $  
- Always double-check units in the final answer!

## 9. Student Corner  

### **Common Mistakes**  
- ❌ Confusing $ 1 \text{ mm} $ with $ 1 \text{ cm} $: Remember, $ 1 \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ mm} $!  
- ❌ Forgetting to move the decimal: When converting $ 12 \text{ mm} $ to cm, always divide by $ 10 $ (not multiply!).  

### **Exam Tips**  
- Always write the unit in your answer (e.g., $ 0.5 \text{ cm} $, not just $ 0.5 $).  
- Check whether the question asks for millimeters, centimeters, or meters—answers vary depending on the unit!  

### **Memory Aid**  
Use the phrase **"Move the dot!"** to remember:  
- To convert mm → cm: Move the decimal **one place left** (divide by $ 10 $).  
- To convert cm → m: Move the decimal **two places left** (divide by $ 100 $).
