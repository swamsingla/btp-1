## 3. Explanation  

### 3.1 Defining *Lakh*  
The term **lakh** refers to the number **100,000**. It is commonly used in Indian contexts to describe large quantities.  

> **Definition**: One lakh equals $100,\!000 $. It is written as $ 1,00,000 $ in the Indian numbering system.  

### 3.2 Numerical Patterns  
The source text provides a sequence of numbers to highlight how numbers grow:  

| Number Type       | Smallest Number | Largest Number |  
|------------------|------------------|----------------|  
| 3-digit          | 100              | 999            |  
| 4-digit          | 1,000            | 9,999          |  
| 5-digit          | 10,000           | 99,999         |  
| 6-digit          | 1,00,000         | 9,99,999       |  

> **Pattern Rule**: Each time you add a digit, the number increases by a factor of 10. For example:  
>

$$
> 999 + 1 = 1,\!000 \quad (\text{adds a zero})  
>

$$  
>

$$
> 99,\!999 + 1 = 1,\!00,\!000 \quad (\text{adds two zeros})
>

$$  

### 3.3 Comparing Numbers with Lakh  
The population of Chintamani in 2011 was **75,000**, while one lakh is **100,000**. To find how much less 75,000 is compared to 100,000:

$$
1,\!00,\!000 - 75,\!000 = 25,\!000

$$  
So, 75,000 is **25,000 less than one lakh**.  

Similarly, the population in 2024 was **1,06,000**, which is **6,000 more than one lakh**.  

### 3.4 Real-Life Application: Days in a Lifetime  
Estu calculates the number of days in a lifetime:

$$
\text{Days} = 365 \times y \quad (\text{where } y = \text{number of years})

$$  
If someone lives for 100 years:

$$
365 \times 100 = 36,\!500 \text{ days}

$$  
This shows why tasting one lakh rice varieties (which would take 36,500 days at one per day) is impossible in a human lifetime!

## 4. Solved Examples  

### Example 1:  
**Problem**: How much less than one lakh is 75,000?  
**Solution**:

$$
1,\!00,\!000 - 75,\!000 = 25,\!000

$$  
**Answer**: 75,000 is **25,000 less than one lakh**.  

### Example 2:  
**Problem**: If a person eats 3 rice varieties per day, how many days would it take to taste 1 lakh varieties?  
**Solution**:

$$
\frac{1,\!00,\!000}{3} \approx 33,\!333 \text{ days}

$$  
Convert days to years:

$$
\frac{33,\!333}{365} \approx 91 \text{ years}

$$  
**Answer**: It would take approximately **91 years** to taste all 1 lakh varieties.  

### Example 3:  
**Problem**: Calculate the population increase in Chintamani from 2011 to 2024.  
**Solution**:

$$
1,\!06,\!000 - 75,\!000 = 31,\!000

$$  
**Answer**: The population increased by **31,000**.  

### Example 4:  
**Problem**: If someone eats 2 rice varieties per day, how long would it take to taste 1 lakh varieties?  
**Solution**:

$$
\frac{1,\!00,\!000}{2} = 50,\!000 \text{ days}

$$  
Convert to years:

$$
\frac{50,\!000}{365} \approx 137 \text{ years}

$$  
**Answer**: Approximately **137 years** would be needed.  

### Example 5:  
**Problem**: Compare 1,06,000 and 1,00,000. How much more is 1,06,000?  
**Solution**:

$$
1,\!06,\!000 - 1,\!00,\!000 = 6,\!000

$$  
**Answer**: 1,06,000 is **6,000 more than one lakh**.

## 8. Student Corner  

### Common Mistakes  
1. **Confusing Lakh with Crore**: Remember, $ 1,\!00,\!000 = 1 \text{ lakh} $; $ 1,\!00,\!00,\!000 = 1 \text{ crore} $.  
2. **Misplacing Commas**: Always write $ 1,\!00,\!000 $ instead of $ 100000 $.  

### Exam Tips  
- Practice converting numbers between standard form and Indian numeral system (e.g., $ 100,\!000 $ vs. $ 1,00,000 $).  
- Use estimation for quick comparisons (e.g., $ 1,\!00,\!000 $ is much bigger than $ 75,\!000 $).  

### Memory Aid  
**"Lakh" = "Life"** – Just as a lifespan has many days, one lakh represents a huge quantity in Indian culture.
