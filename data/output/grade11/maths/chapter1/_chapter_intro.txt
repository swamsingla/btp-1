Welcome to the chapter on Sets! This foundational topic explores how to define and work with collections of objects, introducing essential concepts like sets, their representations, and classifications into finite, infinite, and empty sets. You'll delve into relationships between sets through subsets, universal sets, and Venn diagrams, while mastering operations such as union, intersection, and complement. These tools are crucial for solving complex problems in mathematics and beyond. Get ready to build a strong base for advanced topics!
