# 1.9.1 Union of sets

> **Grade 11** | **Chapter 1: SETS**

---

Given theNC axioms and定义 of set operations, let's proceed step-by-step to find \( A \cup B \).

Given:
\[ A = \{3, 6, 9, 12\} \]
\[ B = \{6, 9, 12, 15\} \]

### Step-by-Step Solution:
1. **Identify Elements in Each Set:**
   - Set \( A \) contains the elements: \( 3, 6, 9, 12 \)
   - Set \( B \) contains the elements: \( 6, 9, 12, 15 \)

2. **Combine All Elements:**
   - Combine all unique elements from both sets \( A \) and \( B \).
   - Notice that the elements \( 6, 9, \) and \( 12 \) appear in both sets.

3. **Remove Duplicates:**
   - Since \( 6, 9, \) and \( 12 \) are common elements, they should only appear once in the union.
   - The unique elements are \( 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 \).

4. **Write the Union:**
   - The union of sets \( A \) and \( B \), denoted as \( A \cup B \), is the set containing all unique elements from both sets.

Therefore, the union \( A \cup B \) is:
\[
A \cup B = \{3, 6, 9, 12, 15\}
\]

Thus, the final answer is:
\[
\boxed{\{3, 6, 9, 12, 15\}}
\]
