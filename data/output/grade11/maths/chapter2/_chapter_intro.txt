This chapter explores fundamental concepts that form the backbone of higher mathematics. Students will delve into relationships between elements of sets, learning how to represent and analyze these connections through ordered pairs and mappings. Key ideas include understanding Cartesian products, which combine sets into coordinate systems, and exploring functions as special types of relations with unique output values. Through graphical representations and algebraic manipulations, learners will develop critical skills for tackling complex problems in algebra, calculus, and beyond. By mastering these foundational tools, students will gain clarity in modeling real-world scenarios and abstract mathematical structures.
