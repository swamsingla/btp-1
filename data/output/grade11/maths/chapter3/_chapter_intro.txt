This chapter explores the foundational concepts of trigonometric functions, bridging geometry and algebra through angles and their measurements. Students will learn to express angles in degrees and radians, understand their relationship to real numbers, and analyze the behavior of trigonometric functions across different quadrants. Key ideas include determining function signs, exploring domain and range, and deriving formulas for sums and differences of angles. These tools form the basis for solving complex problems in physics, engineering, and higher mathematics, emphasizing both theoretical rigor and practical application.
