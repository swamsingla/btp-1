# Conversion Between Fractions and Decimals

Fractions and decimals are two different ways to write the same amount. A fraction shows a part of a whole, while a decimal uses a point to show the same part. Being able to change between them makes maths easier when you are working with money, measurements, or sharing things.

## Converting Fractions to Decimals

When a fraction has a denominator that is a power of 10, the conversion is very simple. The denominator tells you how many equal parts the whole is cut into. For example, $\frac{1}{100}$ means the whole is divided into 100 parts. This is exactly what hundredths mean in decimals.

To change a fraction like $\frac{254}{1000}$ into a decimal, you can break the numerator apart. You write the fraction as a sum of tenths, hundredths, and thousandths.
$$
\frac{254}{1000} = \frac{200}{1000} + \frac{50}{1000} + \frac{4}{1000} = \frac{2}{10} + \frac{5}{100} + \frac{4}{1000} = 0.2 + 0.05 + 0.004
$$
Adding these parts together gives you $0.254$.

You can also use division to change any fraction into a decimal. If you want to divide a number by a power of 10, you can use a simple rule. Let us say you want to calculate $\frac{123}{1000}$. You can write this as $123 \div 1000$.

Step 1: Write the dividend as it is and place a decimal point at the end.
$123.$

Step 2: Count the number of zeroes in the divisor.
$1000$ has 3 zeroes.

Step 3: Move the decimal point from Step 1 left by the same number of places as the count from Step 2.
Moving the decimal point 3 places to the left gives $0.123$.

## Everyday Measurements

Converting between fractions and decimals is very useful in daily life. Imagine you go to the market to buy spices. You might buy 50 grams of cinnamon, 100 grams of cumin seeds, 25 grams of cardamom, and 250 grams of pepper.

To express these quantities in kilograms, you can write them as fractions or decimals.
50 grams is $\frac{50}{1000}$ kilograms, which is $0.05$ kilograms.
100 grams is $\frac{100}{1000}$ kilograms, which is $0.1$ kilograms.
25 grams is $\frac{25}{1000}$ kilograms, which is $0.025$ kilograms.
250 grams is $\frac{250}{1000}$ kilograms, which is $0.25$ kilograms.

## Worked example

Let us use the division rule to convert the fraction $\frac{847}{10000}$ into a decimal.

Step 1: Write the dividend as it is and place a decimal point at the end.
$847.$

Step 2: Count the number of zeroes in the divisor.
$10000$ has 4 zeroes.

Step 3: Move the decimal point 4 places to the left.
$847 \rightarrow 0.0847$

So, $\frac{847}{10000} = 0.0847$.

## Converting Decimals to Fractions

To change a decimal back into a fraction, you look at the last digit of the decimal. The place value of that last digit becomes the denominator of your fraction.

For example, take the decimal $0.678$. The last digit is 8, and it is in the thousandths place. This means the fraction is $\frac{678}{1000}$.

You can also write this decimal as a sum of fractions.
$$
0.678 = \frac{6}{10} + \frac{7}{100} + \frac{8}{1000}
$$
If you add these fractions together, you get $\frac{678}{1000}$.

## Key points

- Decimals are a natural extension of the Indian place value system, representing fractions like $\frac{1}{10}$, $\frac{1}{100}$, and $\frac{1}{1000}$.
- A fraction with a denominator of 10, 100, or 1000 can be written as a decimal by looking at the place value of each digit in the numerator.
- To divide a whole number by a power of 10, place a decimal point at the end of the number and move it left by the number of zeroes in the divisor.
- To convert a decimal to a fraction, use the place value of the last digit to set the denominator.
- The fractions $\frac{254}{1000}$, $\frac{173}{100}$, and $\frac{23}{1000}$ can be written as $0.254$, $1.73$, and $0.023$ respectively by applying the place value rule.
