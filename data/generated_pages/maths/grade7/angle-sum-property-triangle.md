# Angle Sum Property of a Triangle

The interior angles of a triangle are the angles inside the shape, formed by its three straight sides. The angle sum property of a triangle states that the sum of these three interior angles always equals $180^{\circ}$. This property is very useful because it allows you to find the size of an unknown angle if you already know the other two angles in a triangle.

## How to Use the Property

To find a missing angle in a triangle, you simply subtract the sum of the known angles from $180^{\circ}$. For example, if a triangle has two known angles of $50^{\circ}$ and $60^{\circ}$, you first add them together to get $110^{\circ}$. Then, you subtract this sum from $180^{\circ}$ to find the third angle.

## Why the Sum is Always $180^{\circ}$

You can understand why the angles add up to $180^{\circ}$ with a simple activity. A straight line is exactly $180^{\circ}$. Draw a triangle on paper, and carefully tear off its three corners. Now place the three torn corners next to each other on a flat surface, with their pointed tips meeting at a single point. You will find that the three angles fit together exactly along a straight line, with no gaps or overlaps. Since a straight line measures $180^{\circ}$, this shows that the three interior angles of the triangle add up to $180^{\circ}$.

## Worked example

Let us find the missing angle in a triangle where two of the interior angles are $45^{\circ}$ and $70^{\circ}$. 

First, add the known angles together.
$$ 45^{\circ} + 70^{\circ} = 115^{\circ} $$

Next, subtract this sum from $180^{\circ}$ to find the missing angle.
$$ 180^{\circ} - 115^{\circ} = 65^{\circ} $$

Therefore, the missing angle in the triangle is $65^{\circ}$.

## Key points

*   The sum of the interior angles of any triangle is always $180^{\circ}$.
*   To find a missing angle, subtract the sum of the known angles from $180^{\circ}$.
*   This rule applies to all triangles, no matter what their shapes or sizes are.
