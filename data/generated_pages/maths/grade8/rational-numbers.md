# Rational Numbers

Rational numbers are numbers that can be expressed as a fraction $\frac{p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are integers and $q \neq 0$. You might also hear them called numbers expressible as a fraction, or numbers in p by q form. These numbers include all the whole numbers, all the integers, and all the fractions. Understanding rational numbers is important because they are used every day, for example, when you divide a pizza into equal slices or calculate the exact amount of money you need to buy an item on sale.

## Integers and Fractions

Rational numbers are made up of two main types of numbers that you already know: integers and fractions. Integers include all the positive whole numbers, their negative counterparts, and zero. Fractions represent parts of a whole. When you put them together, you get rational numbers. For example, the number 5 is a rational number because it can be written as $\frac{5}{1}$. The number -3 is also a rational number because it can be written as $\frac{-3}{1}$. Even a simple fraction like $\frac{3}{4}$ is a rational number because it fits the definition perfectly.

## Expressing as Percentages

Sometimes, you might need to express a rational number as a percentage. A percentage is just a special kind of fraction where the denominator is always 100. The word 'per cent' comes from the Latin phrase 'per centum', which means 'by the hundred' or 'out of hundred'. So, when you see a percentage like 25%, it means 25 out of every 100. For example, if you have 25 rupees out of 100 rupees, that is 25%.

## Converting Fractions to Percentages

To convert a fraction to a percentage, you can use a simple rule. A fraction is of a unit, while a percentage is per 100. Therefore, to express a fraction as a percentage, you can just multiply the fraction by 100. For example, let us say you have the fraction $\frac{2}{5}$ and you want to express it as a percentage. You can set up the equation $\frac{2}{5} = \frac{x}{100}$. To find $x$, you multiply both sides by 100. This gives you $x = \frac{2}{5} \times 100$. When you do the math, you get $x = 40$. So, $\frac{2}{5}$ is equal to 40%.

## Worked Example

Let us try another example. Suppose you have the fraction $\frac{3}{4}$ and you want to express it as a percentage. You can use the same rule. You set up the equation $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{x}{100}$. To find $x$, you multiply both sides by 100. This gives you $x = \frac{3}{4} \times 100$. When you do the math, you get $x = 75$. So, $\frac{3}{4}$ is equal to 75%.

## Key points

*   A rational number is a number that can be written as $\frac{p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are integers and $q \neq 0$.
*   Rational numbers include integers and fractions.
*   A percentage is a fraction where the denominator is 100.
*   To convert a fraction to a percentage, multiply the fraction by 100.
*   Percentages are used to express quantities as parts of 100.
