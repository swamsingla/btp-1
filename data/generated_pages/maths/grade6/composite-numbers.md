# Composite Numbers

A composite number is a whole number greater than 1 that has more than two factors. While prime numbers only have the number 1 and themselves as factors, composite numbers can be divided evenly by at least one other number. Understanding composite numbers is important because it helps us solve real-world problems, such as figuring out how to pack items into equal groups or cover a floor with the largest possible tiles without any leftover space.

## What Makes a Number Composite

To understand composite numbers, we first need to know what a factor is. A factor is a number that divides another number exactly without leaving a remainder. For example, the factors of 6 are 1, 2, 3, and 6. 

A composite number is defined as a number greater than 1 that has more than two factors. This means it can be divided evenly by numbers other than just 1 and itself. For instance, the number 6 is composite because its factors are 1, 2, 3, and 6. The number 4 is also composite because its factors are 1, 2, and 4. On the other hand, a number like 7 is not composite because its only factors are 1 and 7, making it a prime number.

## Why Composite Numbers Matter

Composite numbers are not just abstract concepts; they have practical uses in everyday life. For example, imagine you are helping to pack rice into bags for a market. You have 84 kg of rice from one farm and 108 kg from another farm. You want to pack the rice into bags of the same weight, and you want to use as few bags as possible. 

To do this, you need to find a weight that is a common factor of both 84 and 108. The factors of 84 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 12, 14, 21, 28, 42, and 84. The factors of 108 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 27, 36, 54, and 108. The common factors are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 12. 

Since you want to use as few bags as possible, you should choose the largest common factor, which is 12. This means you can pack the rice into bags of 12 kg, using 7 bags for the 84 kg and 9 bags for the 108 kg. This is an example of how composite numbers and their factors can help us solve practical problems efficiently.

## Finding Factors of Composite Numbers

To find the factors of a composite number, you can use prime factorisation. Prime factorisation is the process of breaking down a number into its prime factors. For example, the prime factorisation of 90 is $3 \times 3 \times 2 \times 5$. 

Once you have the prime factors, you can use them to find all the factors of the number. For example, if you have the prime factorisation of 225 as $5 \times 5 \times 3 \times 3$, you can combine these prime factors in different ways to find all the factors of 225. 

The factors of 225 are 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 25, 45, 75, and 225. This method is useful because it helps you systematically find all the factors of a number, which can be helpful when you are trying to solve problems involving composite numbers.
